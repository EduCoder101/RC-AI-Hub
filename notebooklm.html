NotebookLM

Your documents. Your questions. Cited answers.

A research assistant that reads everything you give it, and nothing else.

What is NotebookLM?

NotebookLM is not a chatbot. It is a document intelligence tool: you bring the sources, and it works entirely within them. Think of it as a research assistant that has read everything you've given it, and nothing else.

How it differs from Gemini
✓
Answers are grounded in your documents, not the general internet
✓
Cites exactly where in your documents each answer comes from
✓
Can synthesise across multiple documents simultaneously
→
Cannot answer questions beyond what you've uploaded
How it works
📁 You create a Notebook and upload your sources: PDFs, documents, slides, URLs
↓
🧠 NotebookLM reads and indexes everything you've uploaded
↓
💬 You ask questions, request summaries, or generate outputs
↓
📌 Every answer links back to the exact source passage it came from

Which tool should I use?

Use NotebookLM when…

You have documents to interrogate

“I need to find, synthesise, or navigate information across a set of documents I already have.”
  • Querying a syllabus or policy document
  • Synthesising meeting notes or reports
  • Finding information across multiple sources
  • Creating a study guide from course materials
  • Researching College publications or archives
  • Generating a podcast or visual summary of documents
Use Gemini when…

You have a one-off task to complete

“I need help drafting, brainstorming, explaining, or producing something right now.”
  • Drafting an email or letter quickly
  • Brainstorming lesson ideas
  • Explaining a concept in simpler terms
  • Getting a quick second opinion on writing
  • Generating ideas without a specific output format
Use a Gem when…

You do the same task repeatedly

“I have a task I do regularly and want a consistent, reliable tool built for exactly that purpose.”
  • Writing report comments every semester
  • Generating rubrics for assessment tasks
  • Producing differentiated task variations
  • Formatting documents to a consistent structure
  • Drafting parent communications consistently

Getting Started

1

Go to NotebookLM

Visit notebooklm.google.com and sign in with your Roseville Google account.

2

Create a Notebook

Click New Notebook. Give it a clear name, e.g. “Stage 5 English Syllabus” or “Term 3 Meeting Notes.”

3

Upload your sources

Add PDFs, Google Docs, slides, or paste a URL. You can add up to 50 sources per notebook. NotebookLM reads them all.

4

Start asking questions

Type a question in the chat panel. NotebookLM will answer using only your sources, with citations to show exactly where each answer came from.

5

Generate outputs

Use the Studio panel to generate an Audio Overview, a study guide, a briefing doc, a FAQ, or a timeline, all from your sources.

What NotebookLM Can't Do

Understanding the limits is as important as knowing the capabilities. NotebookLM is a specialist tool, not a replacement for Gemini or Gems.

Answer beyond your sources

If the information isn't in a document you've uploaded, NotebookLM will tell you it can't find it rather than generating an answer from general knowledge.

Instead: Use Gemini for questions that don't require your specific documents.

Work from real-time data

It cannot connect to live systems like Edumate, Calumo, or Canvas. You need to export or copy data from those systems and upload the resulting document.

Instead: Export a report, upload the PDF or spreadsheet, then query it.

Remember between sessions

Notebooks persist, but the conversation resets when you close and reopen. Your sources stay, but your chat history does not carry context forward automatically.

Instead: Keep key questions and outputs saved in a Note within the notebook.

Produce repeatable automated outputs

If you want the same task done the same way every time (a rubric, a report comment, a formatted document), a Gem is a better fit than NotebookLM.

Instead: Build a Gem for repeatable structured tasks. Use NotebookLM for research and retrieval.

Process very large files reliably

Very long documents (hundreds of pages) may be processed with reduced accuracy. Breaking large documents into sections or chapters improves results.

Instead: Split large PDFs into logical sections before uploading.

Replace your professional judgement

NotebookLM summarises and synthesises; it does not evaluate or recommend. Always verify important findings against the original source before acting on them.

Instead: Use citations to check any significant claim directly in the source document.

What to Upload (and What Not To)

🔒

Roseville College uses Google Workspace for Education, enterprise tier

This means your data is not used to train Google's AI models and is covered by Roseville's enterprise agreement. General school documents, curriculum materials, and professional resources are safe to upload.

However, some categories of information should not be uploaded to any AI tool. Use common sense: if you would hesitate to email this document to a colleague outside the school, think carefully before uploading it.

✓ Safe to upload
  • NESA syllabus and curriculum documents
  • School policies and handbooks
  • Published College newsletters and archives
  • De-identified meeting minutes and agendas
  • Your own unit plans and teaching resources
  • Professional development materials
  • Anonymised assessment data
✗ Do not upload
  • Documents containing individual student names and results
  • Case notes, counselling records, or medical information
  • HR documents, performance reviews, or complaint records
  • Personally identifiable parent or family information
  • Confidential financial records naming individuals
  • Any document marked confidential or sensitive
