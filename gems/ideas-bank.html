Find Your Own Gem

The best Gems come from your own work. Use these prompts to discover yours.

The best Gems come from your own work. If you have a task that's repetitive, predictable, and always follows the same pattern, it's probably a great Gem candidate. Use these three questions to figure out what yours might be.

1
What task do you do repeatedly that follows a predictable pattern?

e.g., writing similar emails, formatting the same type of document, giving the same kind of feedback

2
What makes it slow, frustrating, or inconsistent?

e.g., you forget steps, the tone varies, it takes longer than it should

3
If it were done perfectly every time, what would the output look like?

Describe the ideal result as if you were showing someone exactly what 'good' looks like.

What makes a good Gem candidate?

  • It has a clear, repeatable pattern
  • The inputs are consistent (you always provide similar information)
  • The output format is predictable
  • Quality matters but the task itself isn't creative judgment
  • You do it often enough that saving 10 minutes matters
