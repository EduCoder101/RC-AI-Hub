The 7-Step Differentiation Framework

This manual follows a research-based 7-step process. You can follow it start to finish, or jump to the step you need.

1

Define Learning Goals

Clarify what students should Know, Understand, Do, and the Skills they'll develop (KUDs).
2

Know Your Learners

Create deidentified class profiles based on readiness, interest, and learning profile.
3

Differentiate Content, Process, Product & Environment

Choose strategies to help all students access and engage with learning. (45+ strategies here!)
4

Align to Learning Goals

Ensure your differentiation still targets the essential learning outcomes.
5

Use the Equalizer

Adjust task complexity using Tomlinson's Equalizer framework.
6

Add Scaffolding

Provide temporary supports that students can use then remove as they gain competence.
7

Implement & Assess

Put your plan into action and use formative assessment to guide next steps.
