Gemini

What would you create if getting started was the easy part?
Gemini is like texting a knowledgeable colleague who’s always free. Describe what you need in plain English: an email, a lesson idea, a rewrite. It delivers in seconds.
Imagine…
  • You need a parent email about camp. Describe the key points and get a polished draft in 30 seconds
  • Tomorrow’s lesson needs discussion questions. Tell Gemini the topic and year level, done
  • You have a half-formed idea for a unit. Use Gemini as a thinking partner: riff on approaches, test angles, and land on something that works
  • A reading task needs adjusting for Year 7. Paste it in, explain the learning goal and where students get stuck, and ask for a scaffolded version
No setup. No training. Just start typing.

Gemini Gems

What if your most tedious task took one click?
A Gem is a custom AI tool you build once, then reuse forever. Set your preferred tone, format, and style. It follows your rules every time.
Imagine…
  • A report comment writer: paste dot points, get polished comments in your voice
  • A quiz generator that matches your faculty’s exact format every time
  • A feedback tool: enter grades, get personalised formative comments
  • A translator that turns policy-speak into clear, parent-friendly language
Some setup. Then one click every time after.

NotebookLM

What if you could ask a 40-page document one question?
Upload your own documents: syllabi, policies, research articles. Then ask questions and get answers with direct citations back to the source.
Imagine…
  • Upload a syllabus and ask “What are the key assessment requirements for Unit 3?”
  • Drop in five research articles and get a summary of the main findings in minutes
  • Load school policies and ask specific questions instead of reading every page
  • Upload your course materials and generate a student study guide automatically
Upload sources first. Then just ask questions.

Differentiation Manual

What if three versions of a resource took two minutes, not two hours?
A step-by-step manual with 79 strategies and 146 AI prompts. Describe your class, paste your resource, and get a ready-to-use differentiation prompt built from research-backed strategies.
Imagine…
  • You have a mixed-ability Year 9 class and a text that half of them can’t access. Use the Prompt Builder to generate foundations, core, and extension versions in minutes
  • A student has adjustment codes you’ve never worked with before. Look them up and get matched strategies with AI prompts ready to go
  • You’re planning a new unit and want to build in differentiation from the start. Walk through the 7-step framework with AI doing the heavy lifting
  • You want to push your top students beyond “more work.” Explore extension strategies that deepen thinking rather than add volume
79 strategies. AI prompts for each. Start with the Prompt Builder.
