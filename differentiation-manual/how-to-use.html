The Full Journey

All 7 Steps

When to use this route:

  • You're planning a new unit from scratch
  • You're planning a sequence of lessons within a unit
  • You're reviewing an existing unit
  • You want a comprehensive differentiation approach

Why all 7 steps:

Whether it's a full unit or a lesson sequence, you work through the complete cycle: clarify your learning goals, understand your students, choose how to differentiate, adjust challenge levels, plan scaffolds, then implement with ongoing assessment.

Examples:

Full unit: Term 2: Persuasive Writing Unit (6 weeks)
Lesson sequence: Introducing fractions through concrete models (3 lessons)
Unit review: Refining my short story unit based on last year's experience

Match the Code to the Strategy

Quick Strategy Lookup

When to use this route:

  • You've seen a strategy code (e.g., P07, C03) and want details
  • You're reviewing past lesson plans with coded strategies
  • Someone recommended a strategy by code
  • You want to quickly jump to a specific strategy

How it works:

Each of the 72 strategies has a unique code. Enter the code (like P07 for Choice Boards or S04 for Graphic Organisers) and instantly access the full strategy details, examples, and AI prompts.

Code format:

  • K01-K04: KUDs Strategies
  • L01-L06: Know Your Learners
  • C01-C10: Content Strategies
  • P01-P13: Process Strategies
  • R01-R10: Product Strategies
  • E01-E04: Environment Strategies
  • A01-A04: Alignment Strategies
  • Q01-Q08: Equaliser Dimensions
  • S01-S08: Scaffolding Strategies
  • I01-I12: Assessment Strategies

What are you trying to do?

When to use this route:

  • You need to adjust a resource you've already got
  • You're planning one lesson and need quick ideas
  • You're ideating support or extension strategies
  • You're planning a sequence or unit from scratch

How it works:

Instead of browsing all strategies, filter by what you're actually doing right now. Need to make a text more accessible? Click "Adjusting a Resource." Planning a unit? Click "Planning a Unit." You'll see only the strategies relevant to that task.

Task types:

  • Ideating Extension (challenge strategies)
  • Planning a Sequence (3-5 lessons)
