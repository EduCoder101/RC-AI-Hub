The gap between knowing and doing

Every teacher can describe good differentiation. But under time pressure, it defaults to "more work" or "less work." This manual bridges that gap with practical frameworks and AI-generated materials you can use on Monday.

Same goal, different pathways

Every student works toward the same learning intention. Foundations strategies build prerequisite skills to access it. Extension strategies deepen understanding beyond it. The continuum keeps everyone moving toward the same destination.

Change the nature, not the amount

Foundations does not mean easy. Extension does not mean more. Effective differentiation adjusts the kind of challenge, not the volume. Fewer questions is a quantitative fix. Sentence starters and graphic organisers are qualitative ones.

AI handles production, you handle judgment

Creating three versions of a resource used to take an evening. With AI, describe your learning intention and class profile, and get foundations, core, and extension materials to review, refine, and use. Minutes, not hours.
