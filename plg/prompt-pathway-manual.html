Why Chain Prompting?

Stop treating AI like a vending machine.

A single prompt produces generic, 'pedagogical beige' output. Chain prompting transforms AI from a shortcut tool into a cognitive co-worker that extends your professional capabilities.

Think of it as supervising a super keen, super green intern. They have broad knowledge but need your guidance, your context, and your professional judgement.
